# Pitch package

Identify the audience, decision, format and submission requirements. Lead with the problem, evidence, proposal, and specific ask. Tie claims, traction, customer examples, metrics, and budget to supplied evidence. Mark forecasts and assumptions and keep figures consistent across files. Review eligibility and deadlines against dated requirements when available. Test whether the recipient can understand the next action from the deliverable alone.

## Adapt to the decision

- For grants, map requirements and scoring criteria to evidence, milestones, budget, eligibility, and the promised outcomes.
- For fundraising, explain the problem, customer, product, evidence of demand, business model, and use of funds. Distinguish observed traction from pipeline and forecasts.
- For sales, identify the buyer's problem, intended outcome, proof, rollout requirements, and the requested next meeting or decision.
- For marketing, connect the audience's problem, positioning, evidence, offer, and call to action. Verify claims across the copy and supporting figures.

## Verify the package

Check word and page limits, filenames, required attachments, dates, presentation order, and consistency among figures. Remove unsupported endorsements and vague asks. Make the recipient's next step explicit.
