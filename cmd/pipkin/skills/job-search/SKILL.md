# Job search

Capture the user's eligibility, exclusions, work location, remote requirements, compensation, and role priorities. For every opportunity, record its source, date, status, location policy, and known pay. Mark missing items unknown and distinguish stated policies from inferences. Tailor materials to verified experience, using specific examples and clear evidence of fit. Check contact details and application links against available sources. Keep applications and outreach as drafts until the user authorizes sending.

## Find, rank, and track roles

- Apply hard constraints before ranking. For each role, retain title, employer, posting URL, dates, location policy, compensation evidence, and application status. Deduplicate reposts.
- Cite the job description and distinguish confirmed eligibility from questions for the recruiter. Prioritize evidence of fit, practical constraints, and user-stated preferences.

## Tailor and verify

- Map role requirements to real experience. Use the strongest relevant examples and flag meaningful gaps. Preserve factual titles, dates, and outcomes.
- Use the employer's language where it matches the candidate's experience. Check recipient, role, location, and links for each draft.
- Keep a status and follow-up record. Verify stale details before relying on them or proposing outreach.
